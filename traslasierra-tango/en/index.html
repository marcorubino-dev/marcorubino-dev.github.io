01 / THE IDEA

Tango can be discovered beyond the stage.

In a conversation, in a song that suddenly makes sense, or by learning the customs of a community.

Traslasierra Tango is a developing concept for exploring this cultural side of tango in the mountain region of Córdoba. We want to understand what each visitor is looking for and shape a possible experience around activities and people who are genuinely available.

03 / THE REGION

Tango meets a different landscape.

Traslasierra has its own pace: mountain scenery, distinct towns and local ways of coming together. The idea is to explore how tango might fit into a visit to the region, without assuming that every town offers the same activities.

We are checking what exists, when it happens and who might be able to help create an experience.
